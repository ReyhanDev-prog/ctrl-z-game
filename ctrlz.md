# Prompt AI - X-TECH Corporate Website

Create a complete, modern, premium corporate website for a fictional technology startup called **X-TECH**.

The website must look like a real-world Silicon Valley technology company, similar in quality to OpenAI, Apple, Tesla, SpaceX, or Anthropic. It should feel authentic and believable rather than looking like a game website.

## General Style

* Dark mode by default
* Futuristic but clean
* Premium corporate design
* Minimalist UI
* Glassmorphism
* Neon blue and cyan accents
* Responsive layout
* Smooth scrolling
* Elegant animations
* High-quality typography
* Modern landing page
* Professional startup branding

The company slogan:

> "Engineering Tomorrow Beyond Reality."

The company description:

> X-TECH is an advanced research and technology company focused on artificial intelligence, quantum computing, neural interfaces, and reality simulation technologies.

# Hidden Story Elements (Very Subtle)

The website should appear completely normal at first glance.

However, include several optional hidden details that players may eventually discover:

* Broken pages returning "403 Restricted Access"
* Hidden research project pages
* Encrypted documents
* Secret employee IDs
* Internal error pages
* Confidential PDFs
* Hidden QR codes
* Unfinished prototype pages
* Archived announcements
* Strange timestamps
* Small glitches
* Missing employee profiles
* Password-protected directories

These elements must feel like accidental leftovers from a real corporate website rather than obvious game puzzles.

The website should be believable enough that players initially think X-TECH is a legitimate technology company.
